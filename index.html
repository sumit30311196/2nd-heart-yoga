Who We Are ?

At 2nd Heart, we believe your heart deserves a second one to care for it. We are a yoga therapy studio dedicated to healing, balance, and holistic well-being. Blending traditional yogic wisdom with modern therapeutic practices, we help you reconnect with your body, calm your mind, and restore your inner rhythm. Whether you're managing stress, recovering from injury, or simply seeking deeper wellness, 2nd Heart is your safe space to breathe, stretch, and heal.

Beginners Yoga Classes – The Most Important Steps

Starting yoga can feel intimidating, but it’s actually the most important step you’ll take.

Our Beginners Yoga Classes at 2nd Heart are designed to make you feel safe, strong, and welcome on the mat. No flexibility required. No experience needed. Just you, showing up.

We break down the basics: foundational poses, breath awareness, alignment, and how to listen to your body. You’ll move at a comfortable pace, ask questions freely, and build confidence with every class.

This is where your journey begins. Learn to flow, breathe, and connect, all while building strength and easing stress. Your second heart starts here.

Get ready to flow, breathe and sweat it out

Take your practice to new heights with our Acro Yoga classes at 2nd Heart. This dynamic fusion of yoga, acrobatics, and Thai massage builds strength, trust, and connection.

You'll learn to base, fly, and spot as we move through playful flows, inversions, and partner balances. Perfect for those with a solid yoga foundation who want to challenge their body, sharpen focus, and laugh along the way.

Just bring your courage, curiosity, and a willingness to defy gravity.

Private Yoga Classes specially designed for you

Your body, your pace, your goals. Our one-on-one sessions are tailored to fit your specific needs, whether it’s injury recovery, stress relief, flexibility, strength building, or deepening your practice.

With personalized attention from our certified therapists, you’ll get customized sequences, hands-on adjustments, and focused breathwork that a group class can’t offer. Practice in-studio or in the comfort of your home, on a schedule that works for you.

This is yoga without comparison, without pressure. Just you, your mat, and progress that’s 100% about you.

Our Therapist

Anjona Mondal

Yoga Therapist

Anjona Mondal blends ancient yogic science with therapeutic care to restore balance in body and mind. An M.Sc in yoga, she brings deep expertise in emotional healing and holistic wellness.

Beyond the mat, Anjona is a back-to-back World Kettlebell Champion Gold Medalist, combining strength, discipline, and mindfulness in every session she leads.

Dhiman Das

Physiotherapist

Dhiman Das focuses on recovery, performance, and pain-free movement. With special knowledge in athletic and gym-related rehab, he helps clients heal from injuries, correct imbalances, and rebuild strength safely.

His hands-on approach blends modern physio techniques with Cup Therapy and Needle Therapy to accelerate healing and restore mobility.

Our Uniforms

For your comfort and safety, we recommend fitted, breathable activewear to all classes at 2nd Heart. Socks are preferred on the mat. Please avoid loose jewelry and strong perfumes. Yoga mats: you’re welcome to bring your own. We suggest bringing a small towel and a water bottle to stay fresh and hydrated.
